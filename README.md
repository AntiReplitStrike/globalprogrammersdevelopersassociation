# Global Programmers' and Developers' Association (GPDA)

Welcome to the GPDA!

We stand together for fair access to programming tools and opportunities to learn, test, and build.

## Our Demands

### 1. Free Coding Time
The right to learn, experiment, test, and build without unreasonable restrictions.

### 2. No Paid Subscriptions
No unnecessary qualifications or artificial barriers should prevent people from participating in development.

### 3. Fair Access to the Platform
Fair and equal access to development tools, platforms, and resources.

### 4. Membership Open to All
Both free and paid users are welcome to join if they feel they are being priced out or unfairly restricted.

### 5. No Exorbitant Fees for Essential Tools
Development tools should remain reasonably priced and accessible to learners and developers.

## Our Purpose

We are not here to attack Replit.
We are here to advocate for fair access, affordability, and opportunities for developers.

**Stand united. Make our voices heard.**
